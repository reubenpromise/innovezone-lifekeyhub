Digital Products

Learn how to create useful digital products, package knowledge and creative work, market them online and build digital assets that can be sold repeatedly.

Create Once. Improve. Deliver Digitally.

Digital products are products that can be created, stored and delivered electronically. They can include educational materials, templates, creative assets, software and many other forms of digital value.

01

Types of Digital Products

📚

eBooks & Guides

Educational books, practical guides, manuals and focused resources that solve specific problems.

📋

Templates

CV templates, business documents, social-media templates, spreadsheets, proposals and more.

🎨

Design Assets

Graphics, icons, presentation designs, social-media assets and other creative resources.

💻

Website Templates

Reusable website layouts, landing pages, UI concepts and other web-design assets.

🎓

Courses & Lessons

Structured educational material designed to teach a skill or help people solve a specific problem.

🧰

Digital Tools

Calculators, generators, utilities and other software-based tools can also become digital products.

💡 Think Value First

A digital product does not need to be complicated. A simple, well-designed template or practical guide can be valuable when it solves a real problem. Focus on usefulness, clarity, quality and honest marketing.

⚠️ Copyright & Original Work

Only sell material you have the right to sell. Do not copy books, courses, graphics, software, templates or other copyrighted material and present it as your own. Check the licensing terms of assets you use in products.
