Turn useful skills into professional services. Learn how freelancing works, develop valuable skills, build a portfolio, find clients and create sustainable freelance opportunities.

Your Skill Can Become a Service.

Freelancing allows people to offer their skills and services to clients without necessarily working as a traditional employee. The key is to develop a useful skill, demonstrate your ability and solve real problems for clients.

🤝 Working With Clients

Professional freelancers normally do not need a client's private account password. Whenever possible, clients should provide appropriate access, invitations, collaborator permissions or temporary accounts instead. Keep client information confidential and separate your own accounts from client accounts.

⚠️ Protect Yourself From Freelance Scams

Be careful with clients who ask you to pay money before receiving work, promise unrealistic payments, request sensitive information unnecessarily or pressure you into unusual payment arrangements. Verify clients and agree clearly on the work, price and delivery before starting major projects.
