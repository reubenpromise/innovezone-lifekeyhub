Affiliate Marketing

Learn how affiliate marketing works, discover different promotional strategies, understand audiences and build a useful online business around products and services you genuinely recommend.

Recommend Value. Build Trust. Earn Commissions.

Affiliate marketing is a performance-based business model where a company rewards a partner for generating qualifying sales, leads or other agreed actions through a tracked referral.

01

Affiliate Marketing Fundamentals

🔗

How It Works

A merchant provides a tracking link or referral system. A marketer promotes the offer and may earn a commission when a qualifying action occurs.

🎯

🛍️

Choose Products

👥

Know Your Audience

Understand the problems, interests and questions of your audience before deciding what to promote.

📊

Track Performance

🤝 Transparency & Affiliate Disclosure

When LifeKey earns a commission from an affiliate referral, that relationship should be clearly disclosed to visitors. Recommendations should be based on usefulness and relevance, not simply on the possibility of earning a commission. Always follow the rules of the affiliate program and the laws or advertising requirements applicable to your audience.

⚠️ Avoid Affiliate Marketing Scams

Be cautious of programs promising guaranteed income, unrealistic commissions or instant wealth. Never assume that an affiliate program is legitimate simply because someone promotes it online. Research the company, read its terms, understand payment conditions and avoid paying large amounts for questionable "guaranteed" opportunities.

Build Trust Before You Build Commissions.

Create useful content, recommend products responsibly and focus on helping people make better decisions. A strong affiliate business is built around value and trust.
