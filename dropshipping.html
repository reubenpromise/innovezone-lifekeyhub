Dropshipping

Learn how the dropshipping business model works, from choosing products and researching suppliers to building a store, marketing responsibly and serving customers.

Sell Without Holding Traditional Inventory

Dropshipping is an e-commerce model where a store can accept customer orders and arrange for a supplier or fulfillment partner to ship products directly to the customer. The seller still has responsibilities such as marketing, customer service, pricing and order management.

01

Dropshipping Fundamentals

🛒

Online Store

Create a clear storefront where customers can discover products, understand pricing and place orders.

📦

Product Selection

Research products based on usefulness, demand, competition, quality, shipping considerations and customer needs.

🏭

Suppliers

Research suppliers carefully and evaluate product quality, fulfillment, communication, shipping and return policies.

📣

Marketing

Use content, search, social media, advertising and other appropriate channels to reach potential customers.

💬

Customer Service

Customers still expect answers about orders, delivery, returns, refunds and product questions.

📊

Business Numbers

Understand product cost, shipping, platform fees, advertising costs, refunds and actual profit.

02

How the Model Works

1

Research

Find a suitable market and product opportunity.

2

Source

Research suppliers and product quality.

3

Build

Create a professional store and product pages.

4

Market

Bring qualified visitors to your store.

5

Order

Customer places an order through your store.

6

Fulfill

Supplier or partner ships the order to the customer.

03

Product Research

🔎

Demand

Look for evidence that people actually want the product instead of relying only on hype.

⭐

Quality

Understand product quality and customer feedback before promoting an item.

🚚

Shipping

Consider shipping time, tracking, cost and delivery expectations.

⚖️

Competition

Study competing stores and determine whether you can provide meaningful value or differentiation.

💰

Profit Potential

Calculate all major costs before deciding whether a product can realistically be profitable.

🛡️

Product Restrictions

Check whether a product has legal, platform, shipping or safety restrictions before listing it.

04

Important Business Numbers

💵

Revenue

Money received from customer orders before subtracting business costs.

📦

Costs

Product cost, shipping, platform fees, advertising, refunds and other expenses.

📈

Profit

What remains after relevant business costs are deducted from revenue.

05

Marketing Channels

📱

Social Media

Create useful product demonstrations, educational content and engaging short-form content.

🎥

Video Content

Demonstrate how a product works and show its practical use honestly.

🔎

Search

Build product and educational content that can be discovered through search.

📧

Email

Use permission-based email marketing to communicate with customers and interested audiences.

📣

Advertising

Paid advertising can be used when the economics, platform rules and targeting make sense.

🤝

Partnerships

Relevant creators or businesses can sometimes help introduce products to suitable audiences.

06

Customer Experience

📨

Order Confirmation

Customers should receive clear information after placing an order.

🚚

Tracking

Provide available tracking information and realistic delivery expectations.

↩️

Returns

Have clear return and refund policies that customers can understand.

💬

Communication

Respond professionally to customer questions and problems.

🤝 Build a Real Business

Dropshipping should not be treated as a shortcut to guaranteed wealth. A sustainable store requires good products, honest marketing, reliable fulfillment, customer service and careful financial management. Test ideas responsibly and improve based on real customer feedback.

⚠️ Avoid Common Dropshipping Traps

Be cautious of suppliers with poor communication, unrealistic delivery claims, copied product images, fake reviews and products that create safety or legal concerns. Also be careful with expensive courses promising guaranteed profits. Research before spending money.

Build. Test. Learn. Improve.

The goal is not simply to open a store. The goal is to understand customers, provide useful products, manage the numbers and build a trustworthy business.
