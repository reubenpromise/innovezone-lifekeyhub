About the Lab

What LifeKey Innovation does

LifeKey Innovation explores practical solutions to everyday problems. We study existing products and technologies, ask how they can be modernized or combined differently, and turn promising ideas into clear product concepts that engineers, manufacturers, entrepreneurs and investors can understand and develop.

Our innovation philosophy

We do not assume that an idea is new simply because we have not seen it before. Promising concepts are checked against existing products, patents and technical research before LifeKey describes them as potentially novel.

Research Integrity

What we know — and what still needs proof

LifeKey does not automatically call an idea a new invention. Existing products, patents, research and engineering constraints must be investigated first.

Related technology exists Solar cooling, evaporative cooling, thermal storage and cold-room solutions already exist.
LifeKey contribution A proposed modular product direction combining multiple preservation approaches.
